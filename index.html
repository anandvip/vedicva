Favourable windows and calm phases, 2026 to 2030

A reference map of when the chart supports money moves, ventures, study, pilgrimage, travel and vehicle decisions, and when it asks for patience. The times are mathematics; the meanings are tradition; the choices stay mine.

Traditional guidance, not financial advice. Nothing here recommends a fund, an amount or a trade. Windows describe the classical timing climate so decisions can be paced calmly.

How to read this reportपठन विधि

Each window layers four classical checks. The dasha says which planets are in charge and which houses they switch on. Gochara (transit) counted from the natal Moon says how the period feels. The same transit counted from the lagna says where in life it lands. Ashtakavarga bindus and vedha (obstruction) refine how strongly a good transit can deliver.

Confidence notes say how strongly the classical layers agree. High means dasha, Moon-based and lagna-based readings point the same way. Medium means one layer disagrees. Low means the reading leans on a single rule. Horizon defaults to December 2026 to December 2030 because no range was set; it can be extended.

Mercury, which rules the money houses (2nd and 11th), runs the current mahadasha.

Classical texts treat a retrograde planet as strong (cheshta bala). Avoiding contracts and purchases in Mercury, Mars or Venus retrograde is a widely followed modern practice, shown here as a caution rather than a rule.

Method, sources and cross-check

How it was computed

  • Swiss Ephemeris 2.10 (Moshier), Lahiri ayanamsa, whole-sign houses counted from lagna and from the Moon, mean lunar node.
  • Vimshottari dasha with 365.25-day years, from the Moon in Mrigashira pada 4.
  • Everything was run twice by two independent paths (sidereal flag, and tropical minus ayanamsa with a different search step). Placements agreed within 0.0001°, and every ingress and station date matched. Clock times differed by minutes, up to about two hours near slow Saturn stations, so this page gives dates only.
  • The run reproduced the 26 Nov 2026 figures: lagna Simha, Moon Mithuna in Mrigashira pada 4, all pratyantar dates, Jupiter into Karka 25 Jan 2027, Saturn into Mesha 3 Jun 2027, mean Rahu into Makara 5 Dec 2026, Mercury retrograde 9 Feb to 3 Mar 2027 and Guru Pushya on 18 Mar 2027.

Classical rules each reading rests on

  • Gochara from the Moon (Phaladeepika, Gochara adhyaya; Brihat Samhita, Grahagochara chapter). Good houses: Jupiter 2, 5, 7, 9, 11; Saturn, Rahu and Mars 3, 6, 11; Sun 3, 6, 10, 11; Venus 1 to 5, 8, 9, 11, 12; Mercury 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11.
  • Vedha (same chapter). Jupiter's good houses 2, 5, 7, 9, 11 are blocked by a planet in 12, 4, 3, 10, 8 from the Moon. Saturn's 3, 6, 11 are blocked by 12, 9, 5. No vedha between Sun and Saturn, or Moon and Mercury.
  • Ashtakavarga (BPHS Ashtakavarga chapters; Phaladeepika Ashtakavarga adhyayas). A transit through a sign with 5 or more bindus in the planet's own table gives good results; 3 or fewer weakens even a good house.
  • Dasha results (BPHS Vimshottari chapters). A period gives the results of the houses its lord rules and occupies, coloured by the antardasha and pratyantar lords.
  • Yogakaraka (BPHS). For Simha lagna, Mars rules a kendra (4th) and a trikona (9th).
  • Muhurta (Muhurta Chintamani tradition). Pushya nakshatra for purchases; eclipse weeks avoided for beginnings.
  • Sade Sati is a later, widely followed tradition built on the classical rule that Saturn in the 12th, 1st and 2nd from the Moon is adverse.

Not sure: exact verse numbers for these rules vary by edition, and the verse text could not be opened online while this was prepared. A human astrologer can confirm them.

Changes since the 24 Nov 2026 cross-check

Downgraded: the "smaller staged moves, 26 Dec 2028 – 29 Mar 2029" window is now Mixed. Mars stays in Kanya, the 4th from the Moon, from 8 Dec 2028 to 28 Jul 2029. That is the classical vedha point for Jupiter's 5th-house transit, so Jupiter's benefit is blocked for the whole stint. Jupiter in Tula in turn blocks Saturn's 11th. A total lunar eclipse falls on the Moon sign on 31 Dec 2028.

Unchanged: main deployment 12 Jul – 5 Dec 2028 and staged moves 13 Apr – 26 Jun 2027 both hold. The 2027 window now notes short fast-planet vedha from 7 May to 25 Jun.

New: 23 Oct – 10 Dec 2029 appears as a secondary window, beyond the earlier horizon.

Settled: with the evening birth time confirmed, Simha lagna stands. Feb – Apr 2027 (Mars pratyantar) and Sep 2027 – Jan 2028 (Jupiter pratyantar) are now read for Simha lagna instead of being on hold.

Points to check with a human astrologer

  • Whether Jupiter's 6 bindus in Kanya and its place in the 2nd from lagna outweigh its weak 4th from the Moon (the main 2028 window rests on this).
  • Whether fast planets (Sun, Mercury, Venus) are counted for vedha. This report counts them but treats them as minor.
  • Mean versus true node near the node sign changes.
  • The Ashtakavarga tables against software such as Jagannatha Hora.
  • Kakshya, divisional charts (D9, D10), Shadbala and the annual Varshaphala are not computed here.
